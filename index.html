BinImg2Vec: Augmenting Malware Binary Image Classification with Data2Vec

Co-authors: TAY Kai Keng, Lee Joon Sern, Chua Zong Fu

BinImg2Vec explores malware binary-image classification with Data2Vec. See the publication for the method, evaluation, and findings.

My involvement

My involvement included gathering malware samples, with exposure to malware repositories and cyber threat intelligence sources during that process.

What I took from the research

The research broadened how I think about malware: beyond the binary itself, other representations can reveal different patterns. That experience continues to shape my interest in cybersecurity and AI research.
